## Context

The app needs accessible interactive primitives — dialogs, dropdown menus, sliders, tooltips, progress — without adopting a full styled component library that would fight the PikaCSS design system (ADR-003).

## Decision

Use reka-ui headless components as the behavior/accessibility layer and wrap them in local `Ui*` components (`UiDialog`, `UiDropdownMenu`, `UiSlider`, `UiTooltip`, ...) that apply the project's PikaCSS styling.

## Alternatives

Not recorded at adoption time; this choice predates EF adoption.

## Consequences

- Keyboard and ARIA behavior come from reka-ui rather than bespoke code.
- Visual identity stays fully owned by the project's styling layer.
- Feature components compose `Ui*` wrappers instead of importing reka-ui directly.
