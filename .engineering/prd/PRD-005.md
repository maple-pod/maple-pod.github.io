## Problem

Music streams from an external resource host, so tracks are unavailable without a network connection even though the app shell itself is an offline-capable PWA.

## User Need

Users want their chosen tracks to keep playing when offline or on an unreliable connection.

## Desired Outcome

Users can save individual tracks to the device, see and manage what is downloaded, and have playback prefer the local copy.

## Success Criteria

- A track can be saved for offline use, and an in-flight download can be cancelled (REQ-008).
- Saved tracks can be listed and removed (REQ-008).
- Saved tracks play without a network connection.

## Non-goals

- Bulk "download everything" of the full catalog.
- Offline availability guarantees for cover art or background images.
