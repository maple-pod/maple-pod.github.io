## Problem

MapleStory's music (BGM) is scattered across game clients and unofficial uploads; there is no convenient, always-available way to browse and listen to it as a normal music library.

## User Need

Players who enjoy the game's soundtrack want to open a web app, pick a playlist, and listen with the controls they expect from any music player.

## Desired Outcome

A browser-based player where all MapleStory tracks are organized into playlists, playback starts in one click, and the player offers standard controls (transport, seek, volume, shuffle, repeat) with cover art and metadata.

## Success Criteria

- Users can browse all tracks and mark-based groupings as playlists and start playback from any track.
- Playback offers the standard control set defined by REQ-003 and queue behavior defined by REQ-004.
- The player is usable on both desktop and mobile browsers as an installable PWA (REQ-009).

## Non-goals

- Hosting or distributing audio files from this repository; assets stay on the external resource host.
- Any server-side features (accounts, social features, server-side search).
