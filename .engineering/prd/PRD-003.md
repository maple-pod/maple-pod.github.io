## Problem

Without accounts or a backend, returning users would lose their favorite tracks, custom playlists, and settings on every visit.

## User Need

Users want to like tracks, organize their own playlists, and have their player and appearance settings remembered the next time they open the app.

## Desired Outcome

A personal library (liked tracks, custom playlists) and preferences (theme, background, volume, shuffle/repeat) kept on the user's device and restored automatically on load.

## Success Criteria

- Liked tracks and custom playlists persist across reloads and browser restarts (REQ-005, REQ-006).
- Player and appearance preferences are restored on load (REQ-006, REQ-010).
- No account or network round-trip is required for any of this data.

## Non-goals

- Cross-device sync or cloud backup; data stays on the device (sharing is covered by PRD-004).
- Multi-user profiles on one device.
