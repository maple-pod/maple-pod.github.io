## Rationale

The slider thumb used the semantic `surface-solid` role after the design-system migration. Once dark-theme semantic tokens were correctly scoped, `surface-solid` correctly resolved to the dark solid surface (`rgb(1, 1, 1)`), which also made the 16px slider thumb nearly black and difficult to locate against the dark player UI. The thumb is an interaction affordance rather than a surface, so it needs its own semantic role that preserves the incumbent light handle across themes.

## Sources

- Maintainer report that the slider thumb is effectively invisible when it renders black in dark mode.
- Production computed-style inspection showing both `Playing Progress` and `Volume` slider thumbs resolving to `rgb(1, 1, 1)` on the deployed dark theme.
- The pre-design-system `UiSlider` implementation used a fixed `#FEFEFE` thumb, establishing the incumbent high-contrast appearance.

## Changes

- Added the `slider-thumb` semantic color token and kept it light in both themes so slider position remains visible without changing the meaning of `surface-solid`.
- Updated `UiSlider` to use the dedicated `slider-thumb` role instead of `surface-solid`.
- Documented the slider-thumb color/contrast contract in `DESIGN.md` and REQ-011.
- Extended the production CSS theme verifier to require a non-dark `slider-thumb` token in the dark theme rule.

## Verification

Result: passed

- `pnpm lint` passed.
- `pnpm type-check` passed.
- `pnpm build` passed, including the production theme-token verifier.
- Production preview computed styles confirmed both progress and volume thumbs resolve to `rgb(254, 254, 254)` in dark mode while the track/surfaces remain dark.
- Browser verification at a 393×852 viewport confirmed the slider thumbs are visibly distinct in the dark player UI.
