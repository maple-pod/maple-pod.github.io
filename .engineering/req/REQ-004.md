## Requirement

Starting playback from a playlist builds a playing queue from that playlist's tracks — in list order, or in shuffled order when random is enabled. The queue is visible to the user, and recently played tracks are recorded and shown as a history list. Queue state remains independent of the concrete playback backend.

## Rationale

A visible queue makes shuffle and repeat behavior predictable, and history lets users return to something they just heard. Keeping queue state above the playback backend allows browser and future native transports to share the same ordering semantics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Playing a playlist replaces the queue with that playlist's tracks; the chosen track starts first.
- With random enabled, the queue order is shuffled; with it disabled, playlist order is used.
- The playing queue can be inspected in the UI.
- Changing playback backends does not require a different queue representation or queue-navigation implementation.
- Recently played tracks appear in a history list, persisted as part of saved user data (REQ-006).
