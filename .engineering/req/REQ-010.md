## Requirement

Users choose a color theme (`light`, `dark`, or `auto` following the system) and a background image for the app. Both take effect immediately and persist as preferences; background assets come from the external resource host.

## Rationale

Appearance is the most visible personal preference; `auto` respects the OS setting by default.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Switching theme updates the UI immediately and persists across reloads (REQ-006).
- `auto` follows the operating system's light/dark preference.
- The chosen background image (including `none`/`auto`) is applied and persisted.
