## Requirement

The app derives built-in playlists from the music catalog — an "All" playlist and one playlist per mark group — plus a built-in "Liked" playlist from the user's liked tracks. Users can create custom playlists, rename or delete them, and add or remove individual tracks from any custom playlist or Liked.

## Rationale

Built-in groupings make the catalog browsable with zero setup; custom playlists and likes are the core of a personal library.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The playlists view lists All, Liked, mark-group playlists, and the user's custom playlists.
- Creating a playlist validates the title: non-empty after trimming and at most 50 characters, with an error message otherwise.
- Custom playlists can be renamed and deleted; built-in playlists cannot be deleted.
- A track can be toggled in/out of Liked and any custom playlist from the UI.
- Custom playlist IDs are namespaced with the `custom:` prefix.
