## Capability
Maintain three playlist concepts: generated All, persistent Liked, and persistent user-created custom playlists with editable membership and ordering.

## Semantics
`All` represents the currently available music catalog and is derived rather than user-managed. `Liked` is a persistent saveable playlist whose membership the user can add to or remove from. Custom playlists are persistent saveable collections with stable opaque identities, user-defined titles, ordered music membership, and user-controlled membership mutation. The identity representation itself, including prefixes or generation algorithms, is not normative.

## Rules
- All, Liked, and custom playlists are stable product concepts.
- Custom playlist titles are validated after trimming, must be non-empty, and must not exceed 50 characters.
- Music membership cannot be changed for All.
- The user can add music to and remove music from Liked and custom playlists; those changes persist.
- Custom playlists support rename, ordering changes, and deletion.
- Reordering a custom playlist may change member order but must not remove playlist members that were not intentionally removed.
- Deleting a custom playlist requires explicit user confirmation before the deletion is applied.
- Custom playlist identity must remain stable and uniquely distinguish the playlist for persistence and lookup, without requiring a particular encoding or prefix.
- Saved playlist references to music that no longer resolves in the current catalog must be removed during readiness normalization.

## Edge Cases
Invalid custom playlist data must not produce a resolvable playlist. Unknown route playlist IDs redirect to the playlist collection. Stale music references are removed so saveable playlists contain only currently resolvable music entries after readiness normalization. A filtered or partial presentation of a custom playlist must not cause unpresented members to be dropped merely because the visible subset is reordered.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/composables/useMusicStore.ts`, `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`, `src/views/Playlist.vue`, `src/router/index.ts`, and playlist UI components. The three playlist categories, 50-character title limit, stable opaque custom identity, stale-reference removal, membership mutation, lossless reordering, and explicit delete confirmation were explicitly accepted during reverse-spec review. The current filtered-view reorder implementation is evidence only and must not override the accepted lossless-reorder contract.
