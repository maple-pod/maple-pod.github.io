## Capability
Maintain the product's built-in, liked, and user-created playlist concepts as durable music collections.

## Semantics
The catalog playlist is derived from available music and is not user-owned. Liked and custom playlists are saveable collections. A custom playlist has a stable unique identity, a user-facing title, ordered membership, and independently mutable membership and order.

## Rules
- Membership mutation is available only to saveable playlists.
- Custom playlist title and identity validity follow the Playlist contract.
- Reordering changes order without removing members that were not intentionally removed.
- Deletion is a deliberate user action and requires confirmation.
- Stale music references are removed during saved-data readiness normalization.

## Edge Cases
Invalid custom playlist data is not resolvable. Unknown route identities do not select a different playlist. A partial or filtered presentation cannot cause hidden members to disappear merely because visible members were reordered.
