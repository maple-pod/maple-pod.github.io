## Legacy Feature notes

## Capability
Provide trustworthy offline readiness for an exact world-map snapshot revision.

## Semantics
The snapshot is ready only when its required runtime resources are complete and verified for the selected revision. Preparation has observable progress, cancellation, failure, retry, removal, and ready states; retained partial data is not a partial ready state.

## Rules
- Readiness is matched to the current snapshot revision and its runtime integrity contract.
- A revision or manifest change invalidates readiness from the prior revision.
- Partial or failed preparation remains not-ready.
- Retrying may reuse retained valid data without weakening the ready boundary.

## Edge Cases
Older, incompatible, cancelled, incomplete, or failed snapshot data cannot appear ready. Removing a snapshot clears its product readiness.

## Legacy Requirement notes: Offline world-map integrity and lifecycle behavior

## Contract
For a selected world-map snapshot, Maple Pod must provide a download lifecycle with observable progress, cancellation, failure/incompletion, direct retry, successful readiness, and removal.

A snapshot may be reported ready for offline exploration only when all runtime resources required by the current exact snapshot revision have been acquired and verified consistently with that revision's manifest/runtime identity. Partial, failed, schema-incompatible, stale-revision, or otherwise unverified data must not be reported as ready.

Download failure must be user-observable. Failed or incomplete preparation must be directly retryable without requiring a prior remove operation. Partial data may be retained and reused by a later retry, but retained partial data must remain not-ready until the full current revision verifies successfully.

## Rationale
A strict ready boundary prevents stale or incomplete world-map content from being represented as dependable offline availability while still allowing direct retry and reuse of valid retained data.

## Verification
Start and cancel a snapshot download; induce a failure and confirm a visible non-ready state; retry directly and complete the download; verify ready only after the exact current revision validates; change the manifest/revision identity and confirm old stored data does not remain ready; remove the snapshot and confirm readiness is cleared.
