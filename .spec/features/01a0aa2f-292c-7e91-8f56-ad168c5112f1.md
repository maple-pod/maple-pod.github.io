## Capability
Remember recent qualified playback occurrences and expose them as persistent, replayable history that stays consistent with the current catalog and playability state.

## Semantics
A playback occurrence qualifies for history after its track remains current continuously for 3 seconds. Qualified occurrences are prepended newest-first, duplicates are preserved as separate occurrences, and persisted history is bounded to 50 entries. Permanently stale references are pruned during readiness normalization. Resolvable entries remain part of history even when temporarily unplayable; playable entries replay through All.

## Rules
- Record only after the same current track remains current for at least 3 seconds.
- Cancel the pending history record when the current track changes before qualification.
- Preserve duplicate occurrences rather than deduplicating by music identity.
- Keep at most the newest 50 occurrences.
- Persist history across application reloads.
- Remove history references that no longer resolve in the current catalog during readiness normalization.
- Keep resolvable but temporarily unplayable entries and present them as unavailable/disabled rather than pruning them.
- Replay must consume existing playability rules rather than bypass them.

## Edge Cases
Rapid track changes under 3 seconds do not create entries. Repeated qualified playback of the same track creates repeated entries. Catalog removal makes matching persisted references stale and eligible for normalization pruning. Temporary offline/playability loss does not erase chronology and must not be confused with stale data.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/composables/useMusicStore.ts`, `src/components/RecentHistory.vue`, and `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`. Numeric history policy, duplicate semantics, stale-reference pruning, and disabled preservation of temporarily unplayable entries were explicitly accepted during product review. Current history rendering assumes references resolve, so normalization behavior is normative intent where implementation is incomplete.
