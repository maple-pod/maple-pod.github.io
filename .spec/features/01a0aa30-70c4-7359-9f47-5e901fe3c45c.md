## Capability
Project one active playback session onto supported keyboard, system-media, and compact auxiliary player surfaces.

## Semantics
Auxiliary surfaces are alternate views or controls for the established player, not separate playback products. They share current music, queue, position, and transport semantics. Active listening may request an exit safeguard; paused playback does not acquire that safeguard solely from having a session.

## Rules
- Supported auxiliary controls delegate to the active Playback contract.
- Keyboard handling respects the accepted focus and event eligibility rules.
- A supported compact auxiliary surface returns to the same session when closed.
- Unsupported platform capabilities do not block primary playback.

## Edge Cases
Interactive or editable focus suppresses the keyboard surface where required. Closing an auxiliary surface must not fork or destroy the queue. Pending transitions remain governed by the established playback session.
