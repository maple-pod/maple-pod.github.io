## Capability
Publish a self-consistent static music/background bundle with explicit current audio representations and build-matched optional loudness analysis.

## Semantics
Each current music entry has stable identity, metadata/title, duration, and an explicit actual audio representation (`file`, `codec`, `container`). Referenced audio, mark, and background assets must exist and match the catalog. Frontend consumption must use the declared representation rather than synthesizing a legacy MP3 path. Loudness analysis remains tied to the same resource build and is independently gated by the Playback loudness contract.

## Rules
- Current-format music entries must declare stable music ID/filename, metadata/title, duration, and actual audio `file` / `codec` / `container`.
- Every referenced published audio asset must exist and match the declared representation.
- Every referenced mark/background asset must exist.
- The background catalog must resolve to published background assets/previews.
- Resource build identity must be present where required by consumers such as loudness compatibility.
- The frontend must not depend on `${musicId}.mp3` as an implicit fallback for current-format entries.
- Legacy MP3 fallback is not part of the desired compatibility contract and should be removed from implementation.
- Acquisition provider details, batch size, retry strategy, container-remux mechanics, and deploy commit strategy are non-normative.

## Edge Cases
A missing `audio` declaration, a declared file that does not exist, or a mismatched catalog/asset representation invalidates the current-format bundle rather than triggering filename guessing. Loudness data may be absent without invalidating ordinary playback-resource publication, but if present it must satisfy the separately accepted build-matching contract before use.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed across both repositories. Product review explicitly accepted the strict current bundle contract and removal of legacy MP3 fallback behavior.
