## Capability
Provide managed offline availability for music items, including individual download and saveable-playlist bulk download, while keeping readiness tied to the current catalog representation.

## Semantics
Offline readiness is not merely the presence of bytes under a music ID. A music item is ready only when saved media is complete and corresponds to the source representation currently selected by the catalog for that identity. Liked and custom playlists may request offline preparation for all current members as a bulk operation; readiness is still tracked per music item.

## Rules
- Download, progress, cancel, remove, visible failure, and direct retry are product-level lifecycle behaviors.
- Failed or incomplete data must not be reported as ready.
- Partial data may be retained and reused by a retry, but this reuse is an implementation optimization and cannot weaken readiness rules.
- A saved representation that no longer matches the current catalog source representation is stale and must not remain ready.
- Liked and custom playlists support bulk offline download for their current membership.
- The generated All playlist is not part of the accepted bulk-download contract.

## Edge Cases
A cancelled, failed, incomplete, or stale saved representation remains not-ready even if some bytes are still retained locally. A retry may reuse retained partial data, but readiness is granted only after the current source representation is complete. Bulk preparation of a saveable playlist does not turn the playlist into a separate downloadable artifact; readiness remains per music item and follows the playlist membership captured by the request.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in music-store offline handling and offline UI components. These semantics were explicitly accepted during Offline reverse-spec review; storage technology, download queue size, and retry implementation remain non-normative.
