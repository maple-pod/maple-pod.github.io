## Capability
Apply persistent appearance preferences for theme and application background.

## Semantics
Theme and background are independent preferences. Theme may follow system appearance dynamically. Background presentation may be absent, automatic, or tied to a selected catalog identity; automatic presentation changes over time among eligible backgrounds. A temporarily unavailable selected identity is a display problem, not a request to rewrite the preference.

## Rules
- Appearance preferences persist independently and are portable saved data.
- System-following theme responds to later system preference changes.
- Automatic background presentation uses the accepted rotation policy.
- A missing selected background renders safely while preserving the stored identity.

## Edge Cases
An unavailable or empty background catalog must not break application rendering. When a missing selected identity becomes available again, the preserved preference may take effect without reconfiguration.
