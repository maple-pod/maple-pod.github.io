## Capability
Apply persistent theme and background preferences with explicit Light/Dark/Auto theme semantics and None/Auto/Specific background semantics.

## Semantics
Theme and background are independent portable preferences. Theme supports Light, Dark, and Auto/System, where Auto follows the operating-system color scheme dynamically. Background supports None, Auto, and Specific catalog identity. Auto rotates among available backgrounds every five minutes. A missing Specific identity falls back visually to no background without rewriting the saved identity.

## Rules
- Persist theme and background preferences independently.
- Theme modes are Light, Dark, and Auto/System.
- Auto/System must track system color-scheme changes.
- Background modes are None, Auto, and Specific catalog identity.
- Auto background must advance every five minutes while eligible backgrounds are available.
- The exact selection order/randomness among eligible auto backgrounds is implementation detail.
- A stale Specific background identity must render safely as no background and remain stored unchanged.
- Appearance preferences are part of portable saved data.

## Edge Cases
If the background catalog is unavailable or empty, Auto cannot produce a concrete background and must degrade safely. If a Specific identity disappears from the catalog, rendering falls back to None without normalizing away the user's stored selection. When that identity becomes available again, it may become effective again automatically.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in app/settings stores and background UI. Five-minute rotation is explicitly normative even though timer implementation details remain non-normative.
