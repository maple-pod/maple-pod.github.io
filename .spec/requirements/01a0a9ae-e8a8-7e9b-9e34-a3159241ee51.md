## Contract
When playback is requested for a valid playlist, Maple Pod must select only a music ID contained in that playlist and must skip items currently unavailable for playback. The playback contract consumes item playability as an input and does not define the connectivity/offline mechanism that determines it.

The session must expose pause/resume, seek within the current track, next, previous, random, volume, mute/unmute, and `off` / `repeat` / `repeat-1` repeat controls. Previous must restart the current music when playback has progressed beyond three seconds; at three seconds or less it must navigate backward in the queue.

Changing random, repeat, volume, or muted state must update active playback behavior and persist the resulting preference across reloads. Persisting current track, queue position, or elapsed playback time across reloads is not required by this contract.

A requested item must not become the current track unless its source resolves and playback starts successfully. Source-resolution or playback failure must leave the session without a successful transition to that item.

## Rationale
The contract separates playback semantics from the mechanism that decides whether a music item is playable. This keeps offline/connectivity policy independently evolvable while guaranteeing that the queue never intentionally selects an unavailable item. Random playback, the three repeat modes, seek, volume/mute persistence, the three-second Previous threshold, and successful-playback transition semantics were explicitly confirmed during reverse-spec review.

## Verification
Exercise playback from a playlist containing both playable and unavailable items; verify requested-item membership checks and skip behavior. Verify seek, next, previous on both sides of the three-second threshold, random toggling, all repeat modes, volume adjustment, and mute/unmute. Reload the application and verify random, repeat, volume, and muted preferences persist. Force source-resolution and playback failures and verify the failed candidate does not become the successfully established current track. No dedicated automated acceptance test for this draft was identified during reverse-spec inspection.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/composables/useAudioQueue.ts`, `src/composables/useAudioPlayer.ts`, `src/composables/useMusicStore.ts`, `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`, and playback UI components. The behavior described above was explicitly reviewed and accepted as part of the playback chain.
