## Preconditions
The application can present an About/context notice and retain browser/device-local onboarding state.

## Main Flow
1. On the first recorded visit, Maple Pod presents the About/context notice.
2. The user reads and dismisses the notice.
3. Maple Pod remembers the dismissal for later normal visits on that browser/device.
4. The user can reopen About from Settings at any time.

## Alternate & Failure Flows
- A new browser/device or a cleared onboarding state receives the first-visit presentation again according to the disclosure contract.
- Portable Saved Data and the narrower saved-data reset do not make the notice disappear from a new device or clear its local dismissal unexpectedly.
- Factory Reset can restore first-visit presentation.

## Observable Outcomes
The disclosure is presented at the intended first-visit boundary, remains manually accessible, and does not repeatedly interrupt a returning user after dismissal.
