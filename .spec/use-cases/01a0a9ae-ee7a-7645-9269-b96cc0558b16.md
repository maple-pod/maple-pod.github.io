## Preconditions
Music metadata has loaded and saved user data is available locally.

## Main Flow
1. The user opens the playlist collection and can access All, Liked, and custom playlists.
2. The user may create a custom playlist with a title.
3. From a music item, the user may add it to or remove it from Liked or any custom playlist.
4. In a custom playlist, the user may rename it, reorder its music, or request deletion.
5. Reordering changes only the order of playlist members and must not remove music that was not part of the reorder interaction.
6. Before a custom playlist is deleted, Maple Pod asks for explicit confirmation.
7. Saved playlist changes remain available through the persisted user-data store.

## Alternate & Failure Flows
- Custom playlist titles are validated after trimming, must not be empty, and must not exceed 50 characters.
- `all` is a generated playlist and cannot receive membership changes.
- Custom playlists have stable opaque identities; prefix and generation mechanism are not part of the product contract.
- Cancelling deletion confirmation leaves the playlist unchanged.
- Unknown playlist IDs resolve as missing; the playlist route redirects back to the playlist list.
- Saved playlist references to music that no longer exists are removed during readiness normalization.

## Observable Outcomes
The playlist list shows All, Liked, and custom playlists; membership, title, ordering, deletion, and persisted state changes are reflected in the UI. Reordering never causes unrelated playlist members to disappear.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/views/Playlists.vue`, `src/views/Playlist.vue`, `src/router/index.ts`, playlist dialogs/menus, `src/composables/useMusicStore.ts`, and `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`. The three playlist categories, 50-character title rule, stable opaque custom identity, stale-reference removal, Liked/custom membership mutation, lossless reordering, and explicit delete confirmation were explicitly confirmed during reverse-spec review.
