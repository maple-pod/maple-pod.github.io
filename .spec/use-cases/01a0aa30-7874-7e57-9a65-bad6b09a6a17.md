## Preconditions
The music catalog is available; compatible loudness data and a capable audio output may or may not be available.

## Main Flow
1. Maple Pod evaluates whether the available loudness data and output path satisfy the Playback loudness contract.
2. When they do, Maple Pod applies automatic per-track loudness compensation during playback.
3. The user listens across track changes without needing to configure compensation manually.

## Alternate & Failure Flows
- Missing, incomplete, stale, or failed analysis data makes compensation unavailable as a whole.
- An unsupported or failed output path bypasses compensation and continues ordinary playback.
- A compensation failure does not make an otherwise playable track unavailable.

## Observable Outcomes
Eligible playback has automatic loudness compensation; ineligible or unsupported playback remains usable through the ordinary path without partial or misleading normalization.
