## Preconditions
Saved appearance preferences are available and the background catalog may be loaded.

## Main Flow
1. The user chooses Light, Dark, or Auto/System theme.
2. Auto/System follows the current operating-system color scheme and responds to later system changes.
3. The user chooses background mode None, Auto, or a Specific catalog background identity.
4. Auto background rotates to another available catalog background every five minutes while the application is in use.
5. A Specific background displays the selected catalog identity when available.
6. Theme and background preferences persist and participate in portable saved-data transfer.

## Alternate & Failure Flows
- If a saved Specific background identity is unavailable in the current catalog, Maple Pod must safely render no background while preserving the saved identity unchanged.
- If the background catalog is unavailable or empty, background rendering must degrade safely without affecting the rest of the app.

## Observable Outcomes
Theme and background choices survive reload. Auto/System visibly follows system color-scheme changes. Auto background changes every five minutes when usable backgrounds exist. A stale Specific identity does not break rendering and can become effective again if that identity later returns to the catalog.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/composables/useAppStore.ts`, `src/components/SettingsDropdownMenu.vue`, and `src/components/AppBackground.vue`. All product-level modes, persistence, stale-background handling, and the five-minute rotation interval were explicitly accepted during review.
