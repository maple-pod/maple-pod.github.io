## Preconditions
Music playback can establish a current track and the current resource catalog is available for resolving history references.

## Main Flow
1. A track remains current continuously for at least 3 seconds.
2. Maple Pod records that music identity as the newest history entry.
3. Repeated occurrences of the same music are preserved as distinct history events.
4. History is bounded to the 50 newest entries and persists across reloads.
5. During readiness normalization, history entries that no longer resolve in the current catalog are removed.
6. The history surface keeps resolvable entries even when they are temporarily unplayable; such entries are visibly unavailable rather than deleted.
7. The user can replay a currently playable history entry through All.

## Alternate & Failure Flows
- If the current track changes before the 3-second qualification period completes, that occurrence is not recorded.
- When capacity exceeds 50, oldest entries are discarded.
- Permanently stale/unresolvable music references are removed during readiness normalization.
- A resolvable but temporarily unplayable entry remains in chronology and must not initiate playback until playability is restored.

## Observable Outcomes
History appears newest-first, may contain duplicate music identities, survives reload, never retains more than 50 qualified occurrences, contains only current-catalog-resolvable references after normalization, and preserves temporarily unplayable entries in a disabled/unavailable state.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/composables/useMusicStore.ts`, `src/components/RecentHistory.vue`, `src/components/RightSidePanel.vue`, and `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`. Product review explicitly accepted the 3-second qualification threshold, newest-first ordering, duplicate preservation, 50-entry capacity, stale-reference pruning, and preservation of temporarily unplayable entries.
