## Actor
A person looking for music in Maple Pod.

## Goal
Narrow a large music collection, locate a known track in context, or return to music heard recently.

## Value
Reach a wanted MapleStory BGM without manually scanning the entire catalog.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/views/Playlist.vue`, `src/components/PlaylistFilterDropdown.vue`, `src/components/RecentHistory.vue`, `src/components/RightSidePanel.vue`, `src/components/MusicDropdownMenu.vue`, and `src/composables/useMusicStore.ts`. Product review explicitly accepted mark filtering, track-location actions, and recent-history replay as two Use Cases under one shared Find Story.
