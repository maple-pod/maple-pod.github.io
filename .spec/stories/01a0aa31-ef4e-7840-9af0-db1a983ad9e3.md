## Actor
A person using Maple Pod, especially on a first recorded visit in a browser/device.

## Goal
Understand what Maple Pod is, where its music metadata/source context comes from, the intended use of collected music, how copyright owners can contact the project, the project's non-affiliation with game development/operation organizations, and which build is running.

## Value
Use the fan-made service with clear context about provenance, intended use, relationship, contact, and build identity without relying on hidden assumptions.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/App.vue`, `src/components/AboutDialog.vue`, and Settings access. Product review explicitly accepted one-time proactive first-visit presentation, device/browser-local dismissal persistence until Factory Reset or storage clearing, all six disclosure content categories, and semantic-not-verbatim wording.
