## Actor
A Maple Pod resource maintainer.

## Goal
Build and publish static music, visual, analysis, and world-map resources with enough identity, compatibility, provenance, and integrity information for Maple Pod to consume them safely.

## Value
Let the application evolve independently from resource acquisition while rejecting incomplete, stale, incompatible, or internally inconsistent published data.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed across the sibling `maple-pod/resources` repository, its tests/README, and frontend consumers. Product review explicitly accepted strict current-format music bundle consistency, removal of legacy MP3 fallback, explicit versioned World Map compatibility without freezing today's schema numbers, removal of the unversioned legacy alias, strict full selectability, revalidation demotion, snapshot-bound canonical provenance, and explicit unresolved topology references.
