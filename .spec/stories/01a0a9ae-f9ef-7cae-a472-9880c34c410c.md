## Actor
A person sharing or receiving a Maple Pod music selection.

## Goal
Share a music item or saveable playlist through a Maple Pod link so the receiver can reconstruct the intended selection.

## Value
Transfer a music selection without requiring the receiver to manually recreate it.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/components/MusicDropdownMenu.vue`, `src/components/MusicPlayer.vue`, `src/components/PlaylistDropdownMenu.vue`, `src/router/index.ts`, and `src/utils/common.ts`. During reverse-spec review, music sharing and playlist sharing were explicitly accepted as one user-value story. This intent was explicitly accepted during reverse-spec review.
