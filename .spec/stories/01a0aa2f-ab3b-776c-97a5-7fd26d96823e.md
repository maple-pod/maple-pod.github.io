## Actor
A person with personalized Maple Pod state.

## Goal
Back up, transfer, restore, selectively reset, or fully reset Maple Pod-owned local state.

## Value
Avoid being locked to one browser storage instance, recover a known personal setup, and deliberately clear either saved personal data or all Maple Pod-owned local state at an appropriate scope.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/components/SettingsDropdownMenu.vue`, `src/router/index.ts`, `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`, saved-data schemas, world-map selection persistence, offline storage, and onboarding state. Product review explicitly accepted a portable saved-data boundary distinct from a two-level reset model: Reset Saved Data and Factory Reset.
