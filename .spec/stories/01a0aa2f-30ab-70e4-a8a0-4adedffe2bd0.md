## Actor
A person using Maple Pod who wants selected content to remain usable without network connectivity.

## Goal
Prepare selected music and world-map content for offline use, understand its readiness, and manage that offline content over time.

## Value
Keep important Maple Pod experiences available when connectivity is unavailable or unreliable without mistaking incomplete or stale data for ready offline content.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed across music offline storage, playlist bulk download UI, world-map offline storage, and offline-aware playback. Review explicitly accepted one shared Offline Story for music and World Map, a full download/progress/cancel/remove lifecycle, observable failure, direct retry, and strict readiness semantics as normative behavior.
