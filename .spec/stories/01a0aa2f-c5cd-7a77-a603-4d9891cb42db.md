## Actor
A person exploring MapleStory places and their music.

## Goal
Browse versioned MapleStory world-map snapshots, navigate their topology, inspect places, and play associated BGM.

## Value
Connect MapleStory BGM with the game locations and historical map context where it appears.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/views/WorldMap.vue`, `src/composables/useWorldMaps.ts`, `src/schemas/WorldMaps.ts`, and the resources repo world-map pipeline. Product review explicitly accepted versioned selectable snapshots, persistent/portable remembered selection, browser-locale display enrichment, and explicit unresolved topology rather than invented navigation, manifest-ready snapshot commit semantics, and reuse of the normal Playback contract.
