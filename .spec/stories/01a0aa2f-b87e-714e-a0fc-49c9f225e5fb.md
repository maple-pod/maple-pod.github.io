## Actor
A person using Maple Pod regularly.

## Goal
Choose and keep a visual appearance that fits system preference or personal taste.

## Value
Make Maple Pod comfortable and recognizable without changing music or playlist content.

## Reverse-spec evidence
Observed in `src/components/SettingsDropdownMenu.vue`, `src/composables/useAppStore.ts`, `src/components/AppBackground.vue`, and `src/composables/useSavedUserData.ts`. Product review explicitly accepted persistent Light/Dark/Auto theme modes and None/Auto/Specific background modes, including five-minute auto-background rotation.
