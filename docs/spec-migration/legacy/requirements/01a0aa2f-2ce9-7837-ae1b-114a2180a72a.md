## Contract
Maple Pod must maintain a persistent recent playback history. A playback occurrence must be recorded only when the same track remains current continuously for at least 3 seconds. Recorded occurrences must be ordered newest-first, repeated occurrences of the same music identity must be preserved, and only the newest 50 occurrences may be retained.

Changing the current track before the 3-second qualification threshold completes must prevent that occurrence from being recorded. Exceeding the capacity must discard the oldest occurrences first.

During readiness normalization, persisted history references that no longer resolve in the current resource catalog must be removed. A history reference that still resolves but is temporarily unplayable because of current connectivity/playability state must remain in history and be presented as unavailable/disabled rather than removed.

A resolvable playable history entry must be replayable through All and must continue to obey the product's playability rules. Temporarily unplayable entries must not bypass that boundary.

## Rationale
The qualification threshold avoids filling history with tracks the user merely passed through, while duplicate occurrences preserve actual listening chronology. Separating permanent stale references from temporary playability loss keeps persisted data clean without destroying valid listening history during transient conditions.

## Verification
Switch tracks before and after 3 seconds and confirm only qualified occurrences are recorded. Play the same track repeatedly and confirm duplicate qualified occurrences remain. Exceed 50 occurrences and confirm oldest entries are evicted. Reload and confirm persistence. Inject a stale catalog ID and confirm readiness normalization removes it. Make a resolvable entry temporarily unplayable and confirm it remains visible but cannot be replayed until playability returns.
