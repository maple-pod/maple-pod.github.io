## Capability
Maintain a durable, replayable chronology of qualified recent listening occurrences.

## Semantics
History records listening occurrences rather than unique music identities. Qualified occurrences are ordered newest-first, bounded by the accepted history capacity, and retained across reloads. Catalog staleness and temporary playability loss are different states.

## Rules
- An occurrence is recorded only after it satisfies the Recent History qualification policy.
- A current-track change before qualification cancels that occurrence.
- Repeated qualified listening remains represented as repeated occurrences.
- Stale references are pruned during readiness normalization; resolvable but temporarily unplayable references remain available as unavailable history.
- Replay uses the ordinary playability and playback contracts.

## Edge Cases
Rapid transitions do not create qualified history. Catalog removal may prune an entry without erasing other chronology. Temporary unplayability must not erase a resolvable occurrence or bypass playback rules when replay is attempted.
