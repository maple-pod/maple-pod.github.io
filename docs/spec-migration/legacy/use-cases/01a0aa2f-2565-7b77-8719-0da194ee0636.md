## Preconditions
Playback can establish current music and the current catalog can resolve saved history entries.

## Main Flow
1. The user listens to music long enough for an occurrence to qualify under the Recent History contract.
2. Maple Pod adds the qualified occurrence to the recent-history view.
3. The user browses recent entries and may replay an entry that is currently playable.
4. Maple Pod keeps the history available after reload and preserves repeated listening occurrences as chronology.

## Alternate & Failure Flows
- A track change before the qualification condition is met does not create an occurrence.
- Entries that are permanently stale are removed according to the history contract.
- A resolvable but temporarily unplayable entry remains visible as unavailable and cannot bypass normal playability rules.

## Observable Outcomes
Recent History shows qualified entries newest-first, retains repeated occurrences, persists across reloads, and distinguishes unavailable entries from entries removed as stale.
